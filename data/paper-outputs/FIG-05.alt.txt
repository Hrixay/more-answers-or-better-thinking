Three panels of whole-answer accuracy against puzzle length, for chains, pieces and both pooled, in the length sweep on other puzzles. Each model's accuracies are joined by a line in length order, with a 95% bar on each point (hollow markers in the pieces panel); Sonnet-medium and Opus-medium are shifted a little to the left and to the right of each length to keep the markers and bars of one length apart, and Haiku-medium stands at its true length. A grey dashed line with a light band is the reference q^N, built from Haiku-medium's per-piece accuracy; it is labelled once, in panel A, Haiku-medium q^N (not fitted), with a short grey leader from the label to the line. Panel B's title says its markers are hollow. Haiku-medium pooled accuracy falls from 55.5% at 4 steps to 18.0% at 16 steps, while Opus-medium is at 97.5% and 100.0% and Sonnet-medium goes from 87.5% to 65.0%. The reference line, the independent-pieces line, reaches 20.4% at 16 steps. The points that the format check flags on chains are named in the caption. The chains' own per-step line (post hoc†) is not drawn; its values, with their placeable counts, are in this figure's data file. Study 1b's Haiku-max and Opus-medium and Pilot 1's markers at 24 steps are in the appendix version of this figure. Bars drawn from exact 95% intervals, post hoc, where a bootstrap interval collapsed to a point: length-sweep Opus-medium at 6 steps, pooled over shapes; length-sweep Opus-medium at 16 steps, pooled over shapes.
