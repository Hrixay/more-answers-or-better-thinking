Two panels of whole-answer accuracy against list cost per answer on a log axis, from Study 1b's effort sweep, with each model's settings joined in effort order and each point labelled with its setting: 6 steps (A), for all three models, and 16 steps (B), for Haiku and Opus-medium. Both panels share one accuracy axis, from zero to one hundred percent. Points pool chains and pieces. A dashed line marks Opus-medium's accuracy in each panel. In A the markers for Haiku-low and Haiku-medium, for Sonnet-low and Sonnet-medium, and for the Opus settings low to xhigh with Sonnet-max overlap, so their values are in TAB-12. At 6 steps Haiku accuracy rises with effort from 27.5% with thinking off, through 52.8% at low and 54.5% at medium, to 95.8% at max, while its cost per answer rises from 0.000927 to 0.00419 US dollars.
