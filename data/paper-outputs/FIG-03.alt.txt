Three panels for Haiku-max votes, with the vote size k on the horizontal axis, a log scale from 1 to 10 samples: A at 16 steps; B at 6 steps and C at 12 steps, both post hoc. In each panel the vote pooled over chains and pieces is a thick line with a 95% band, which starts at the first vote size from the setting's own stamped one-sample interval; one Opus-medium answer is a dashed line, drawn over the vote line where the two meet, with a diamond and its 95% interval as a bar at the right edge; a tick below the horizontal axis, in Opus-medium's colour, marks the cost-matched vote size, labelled in B and C. In panel A the vote size tested in Test B2, which is also the cost-matched size at 16 steps, is the one larger marker, with its label. A second horizontal axis above panel A gives the vote's list cost per answer as a share of one Opus-medium answer's price, at vote sizes 1, 3, 5 and 10: 0.15, 0.45, 0.75 and 1.49; Test B2's label gives its share, 0.894. The accuracy axes start at 88, not at zero. Panel A: whole-answer accuracy, pooled over chains and pieces, rises from 92.6% at one sample to 99.4% at 10 samples. At k = 6 (Test B2, confirmatory, the cost-matched size) it is 98.4%, 95% interval 97.1% to 99.5%, beside 97.8% for one Opus-medium answer (95% interval 96.3% to 99.1%). Cost, on the second axis of panel A: the list cost of the vote per answer is 0.0112 US dollars at one sample and 0.0670 at k = 6, against 0.0750 for one Opus-medium answer, pooled over shapes: a spend share of 0.894.
